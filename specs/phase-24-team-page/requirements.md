# phase-24-team-page — Requirements

## Status

completed

## Objective

Dedicated page per team (`/teams/[id]`) with squad, group, tournament matches,
and model rating. Gives the app content depth and a natural destination when the user
wants to know more about a specific national team.

## Context

The app has the group standings table (`/groups`) and the fixture list (`/fixtures`),
but there is no page focused on a specific team. The data already exists:
- Squad: `lib/data/squads.json` (generated by `pnpm precompute:squads`).
- Matches: `loadFixtures()` filtered by `homeTeamId` or `awayTeamId`.
- Model rating: `Team.attackStrength` and `Team.defenseStrength` from
  `lib/agents/static-teams.ts`.
- Group and position: from `buildStaticTeams()`.

## Scope

- `/teams/[id]` route — Server Component with ISR (revalidate 3600).
- Sections:
  1. **Header**: name, flag, group.
  2. **Model rating**: attackStrength and defenseStrength visualized as bars
     relative to the average (1.0).
  3. **Tournament matches**: list of fixtures where the team plays, with result
     if already played or prediction if scheduled.
  4. **Top squad**: top players from the team by `goalsPerMinute` from
     `squads.json` (max. 8 players).
- Links from the group standings table and from fixture headers.

## Out of Scope

- Real-time tournament statistics (goals scored, cards). Future phase.
- Results history outside World Cup 2026.
- Team comparison (future phase).
- Tactical lineup or formation.

## Requirements

1. `/teams/[id]` renders without errors for any tournament team.
2. The header shows the team name with flag and group.
3. The model rating is visible with some visual representation.
4. Team fixtures are listed in chronological order.
5. The top squad shows at least the 5 best attackers/midfielders according to
   the model.
6. Team name headers on fixture pages link to `/teams/[id]`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `/teams/1` (United States) renders correctly.
- [ ] Header with name, flag, and group visible.
- [ ] Attack and defense rating visible.
- [ ] List of team matches with results/predictions.
- [ ] Top squad with players and projections.
- [ ] Links from fixture headers work.
- [ ] `pnpm tsc --noEmit` passes.
- [ ] `pnpm build` passes.

## Risks and Assumptions

- `squads.json` may have players without a defined position or with very low
  `goalsPerMinute`. The top squad must filter by eligibility (same logic as the model).
- Team IDs in the URL must match the canonical ID from `teams-seed.ts`,
  not the football-data.org ID.
- If the team does not exist, the route must call `notFound()`.
